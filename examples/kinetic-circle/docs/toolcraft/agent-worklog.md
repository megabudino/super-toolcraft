# Implementation Worklog

Active change: template-release-2026-09-09

## Status

Mode: product

Kinetic Circle is a procedural animated product app built around one centered 3D mosaic relief whose point layout (rings, sunflower, spiral arms, arcs), contour, deformation depth, repetition, palette source (curated presets or custom colors), color-distribution mode, per-dot style (shape mask, taper, additive glow), motion family (seven seamless modes plus sparkle), and orientation can all be configured, with one-click curated randomize actions. It uses a WebGL canvas renderer, a starter-style orientation gizmo, finite and infinite canvas modes, Toolcraft playback timeline, persistent creative settings, and still/video export.

## Decisions

### Renderer

- Decision: Persistent WebGL 2 point-sprite preview with one deterministic CPU-generated 3D volume arranged by four bounded layouts (rings, phyllotaxis, spiral arms, arcs), seven CPU color-distribution modes over preset or custom palettes, separate ridge/helix/dome depth components, GPU-only signed dome bend, five fragment-shader point masks with optional additive glow and taper, typed nonlinear response, seven radial/tangential/axial motion equations plus per-dot sparkle, combined-extreme fitting, explicit canvas-Y-down to right-handed-world-Y-up conversion, orientation projection, and matching WebGL export frames.
- Reason: Thousands of animated samples remain a high-count pixel workload; layout, shape, spread, twist, and coloring rebuild one bounded geometry buffer, while dot style, signed bend, perspective, dynamics, motion families, sparkle, and 3D orientation update through uniforms. The explicit coordinate conversion keeps the app renderer aligned with the runtime gizmo contract.
- Evidence: `src/app/kinetic-mosaic-renderer.tsx`, the typed Renderer Layer and Render Pipeline inventories in `src/app/app-performance.ts`, form acceptance, and dense preview/viewport scenarios.

### Timeline

- Decision: Use the Toolcraft playback timeline with an 8-second product-derived default loop.
- Reason: Product animation and Export Video require user-facing play, pause, scrub, duration, loop, and export-time ownership; integer motion cycles keep all four shader modes seamless and forward-only.
- Evidence: `appSchema.panels.timeline.mode = "playback"`, `appTransferMode.animationIntent`, `getToolcraftTimelineLoopProgress`, and timeline browser acceptance.

### Layers

- Decision: Do not enable Layers.
- Reason: The product has one procedural object, so selection, ordering, visibility, and per-layer editing would add no real product behavior.
- Evidence: `panels.layers` is omitted and no `selectedLayer.*` targets exist.

### Controls

- Decision: Use built-in selects, discrete/continuous sliders including signed `Z bend` and `Taper`, one radial `rangeSlider`, colors, the Background switch, the starter `orientationGizmo`, runtime-owned `canvas.infinity`, a section-level `actions` control for the two curated randomize commands, and panel actions grouped as Setup, Variations, Shape, Volume, Dot Field, Dot Style, Mosaic Palette, Coloring, Motion, Dynamics, Background, Image Export, and Video Export; layout-specific (`Arms`, `Arc coverage`) and preset-specific (five custom colors) controls hide through `visibleWhen`; every creative slider follows the explicit response contract in `product-spec.md`.
- Reason: Inner/outer radius is one lower/upper range value; layout, dot shape, palette preset, and color mode are finite choices; randomize is a local command pair rather than product delivery; every other setting maps directly to a numeric, color, boolean, finite option, pose, canvas mode, or final action value model already owned by Toolcraft controls and commands.
- Evidence: `starterControlSectionInventory`, `src/app/app-schema.ts`, runtime target injection, the viewport gizmo layer, and control/canvas-handle acceptance rows.

### Export

- Decision: Export PNG/JPG through `createToolcraftPngExportCanvas`; export video from deterministic timeline frames at `getToolcraftVideoExportSize` dimensions with supported MIME fallback and frame progress.
- Reason: Standard helpers preserve background alpha and exact 2K/4K/8K image or Current/4K video dimensions.
- Evidence: `exportMosaicImage`, `exportMosaicVideo`, Image Export/Video Export controls, and sticky panel action acceptance.

### Performance

- Decision: Cache geometry and shader resources, keep `Z bend`/perspective/dynamics/orientation uniform-only, fit only compounded upper extremes, coalesce background pan/zoom work, and use a temporary 1× live backing for continuous orbit/slider gestures with an immediate trailing full-quality frame.
- Reason: The hard limit is full radial span, density 100, every Z/projection/dynamics maximum, and selected 2× backing. Temporary interaction backing removes intermittent high-resolution stalls without reducing the exposed range, saved quality, playback, or export fidelity.
- Evidence: `src/app/app-performance.ts`, the agent-browser measurements in Iteration 7, the typed interaction invalidation map, and dense preview/playback/pan/zoom scenarios.
